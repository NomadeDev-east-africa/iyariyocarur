Welcome to C&C

At Ciyaar & Caruur (or C&C as we lovingly call it), we've created a magical world where children can laugh, play, and grow. Our name means "Play and Children" in Somali, and it perfectly captures our mission: to provide a safe, joyful space for every child who walks through our doors.

With 6 letters in each word, C&C rolls off the tongue and represents our commitment to balance: between fun and safety, between energy and calm, between playtime and family time.

Founded with love in Hargeisa, we've become the go-to destination for families seeking quality indoor entertainment for children aged 1 to 10 years.
