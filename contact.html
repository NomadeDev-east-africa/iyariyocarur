Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to the most commonly asked questions

From what age can children enter?

We welcome children aged 0-12 years. Toddlers have their own dedicated space with age-appropriate games.

Do I need to book in advance?

For birthdays, booking is mandatory. For regular visits, it is recommended on weekends and during school holidays.

Do parents need to stay?

Yes, adult supervision is required. We have a lounge area with wifi and drinks to welcome you.

Do you offer food?

Yes, we have a snack bar with drinks, snacks and snack packages. For birthdays, we offer full meals.

Are shoes allowed?

For hygiene and safety reasons, children must wear socks in the play structures. Non-slip socks are recommended.

How to cancel a reservation?

You can cancel for free up to 48h before your reservation. After this period, fees may apply.
